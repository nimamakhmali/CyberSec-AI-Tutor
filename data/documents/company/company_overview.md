# Company Overview - SecureNet Solutions

## About Us

SecureNet Solutions is a leading cybersecurity consulting and managed security services provider founded in 2015. We specialize in helping organizations build resilient security postures through proactive defense strategies, continuous monitoring, and incident response.

## Our Mission

To empower organizations with the knowledge, tools, and expertise needed to defend against evolving cyber threats while maintaining business continuity and regulatory compliance.

## Services

### Security Assessment & Testing
- **Penetration Testing**: Network, web application, mobile, and wireless
- **Vulnerability Assessment**: Automated scanning with manual verification
- **Red Team Operations**: Adversary simulation exercises
- **Purple Team Exercises**: Collaborative attack/defense training
- **Cloud Security Assessment**: AWS, Azure, GCP configuration review

### Managed Security Services
- **24/7 SOC Monitoring**: Continuous threat detection and response
- **Managed Detection & Response (MDR)**: Endpoint and network threat hunting
- **SIEM Management**: Log aggregation, correlation, and alert tuning
- **Vulnerability Management**: Continuous scanning and remediation tracking
- **Threat Intelligence**: Customized feeds and adversary profiling

### Incident Response & Forensics
- **Emergency Response**: 4-hour on-site/remote response SLA
- **Digital Forensics**: Disk, memory, and network forensics
- **Malware Analysis**: Static and dynamic analysis capabilities
- **Post-Incident Review**: Lessons learned and improvement planning

### Advisory & Compliance
- **Security Program Development**: Strategy, roadmap, and maturity assessment
- **Compliance Support**: SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, PCI DSS, GDPR
- **Risk Assessment**: Business impact analysis and risk quantification
- **Security Architecture Review**: Zero trust, cloud, and hybrid environments
- **Security Awareness Training**: Phishing simulations and role-based training
